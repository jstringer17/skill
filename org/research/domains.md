# Domain and brand naming

- Chosen: **lumovaco.com** ($16, available when checked on 2026-10-20). The storefront now redirects to www.lumovaco.com, so it is attached to the store. Shopify said the user gets a $20 credit toward a plan if they buy a domain.
- Brand name on pages: Lumova. Rule from the user: names must be niche-agnostic, like Averin/Avorin, Lumova, Lexivo, Luminyx; "Omerta" was rejected (mafia meaning).
- About 200 candidate names were checked with Shopify's domain tool. Nearly every exact short `.com` was taken. Open `.com` names found at $16 included: viozora, venalune, vanilume, vanihaus, vanelyra, lorviora, silvabloom, roostlark, frondfolk, holvani, mistmoor, nivarise, glixly, aurinyx, verinyx, lyvanyx, pineandnook, nestandweave, junijade, crezimo, coveora, wrenlume, glowwren, velvetfrond, getavorin, avorinhome, getlumova, lumovaco, lexivohome, lexivoco, tryvizora, vizorahome, omertahome, omertaco.
- Taken as `.com`: vizora, avorin, lumova, lexivo, omerta, luminix, clearspring, fernhaus, nestlark, and most short invented names.
- Trademark checks were not done. Search USPTO for "Lumova" before spending on ads (lumova.com belongs to someone else).
