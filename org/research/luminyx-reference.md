5. "Luminyx Product Highlights" on a mint band: product centered with four callouts (Single Blade, Safety Guard, Weighted Handle, Knurled Grip).
6. "Why one blade" comparison: 30c per blade vs $4 per cartridge, multi-blade vs single-blade diagrams.
7. "How to shave": four steps (Prepare, Find the angle, Let it glide, Finish) on a mint band.
8. "The ritual" lifestyle section with a Shop Now button.
9. FAQ in two columns (heading left, accordion right): Will I cut myself? Which blades fit? How often change the blade? Bikini line and underarms? Travel? What if it isn't for me? (the last answer was an unedited template).
