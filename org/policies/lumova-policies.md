## Refund policy

**30-day returns**

If you are not happy with your order, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund.

What can be returned
- Unused items in their original packaging.
- For hygiene reasons, opened blades, opened skincare items, and installed filter cartridges cannot be returned.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items
If your item arrives damaged, does not work, or is not what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, and you do not need to return the item.

How refunds work
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after we receive your return or approve your claim. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or defective.
